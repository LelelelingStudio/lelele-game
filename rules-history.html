游戏守则历史版本Rules History

每个版本对应一份守则原文。改版时旧版本不删除，只归档，方便作者对照自己投稿时适用的规则。 Every version keeps its own copy. Old versions are never deleted — they are archived so authors can check the rules that applied when they submitted.

当前生效In effect now

  • 5.1.0
    游戏守则 5.1.0（现行）Game Rules 5.1.0 (current)
    2026 年 11 月 28 日生效Effective 28 Nov 2026
    核心变化：自 2026 年 11 月 28 日起，新投稿的游戏须接入「了了了游开发者 SDK」。5.0.0 版要求的所有游戏必须具备用户协议、隐私政策与服务条款，继续有效。 Key change: games submitted on or after 28 Nov 2026 must integrate the Lelele Game Developer SDK. The 5.0.0 requirement — every game must provide a user agreement, privacy policy and terms of service — still applies.
