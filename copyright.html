版权与侵权投诉指引

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 18 日

了了了游（以下简称“本平台”）高度重视知识产权保护。若您认为本平台展示的某款游戏侵犯了您的合法权益，请按照本指引向我们提交正式的侵权通知。

一、通知要求

根据《中华人民共和国民法典》第 1195 条，您的侵权通知应当包含以下内容：

  • 权利人身份证明：包括您的姓名（或名称）、身份证/营业执照复印件、联系方式（电话、邮箱、地址）。
  • 权属证明：包括但不限于著作权登记证书、商标注册证、原创作品底稿、首次公开发表证明等能够证明您拥有相关权利的材料。
  • 侵权内容定位：请明确指出涉嫌侵权的游戏名称，以及该游戏在本平台的具体链接（URL）。
  • 侵权具体说明：简要说明涉嫌侵权的具体内容及理由。
  • 真实性声明：请声明您通知中的陈述真实、准确，并愿意承担因不实通知产生的法律责任。
⚠️ 注意：若您发出的通知不完整或不符合法定要求，本平台将暂缓处理。因错误通知导致被投诉方或本平台遭受损失的，权利人应依法承担赔偿责任。

二、提交方式

请将上述材料打包发送至本平台指定邮箱：

leleleling@2980.com

邮件标题请注明：【侵权投诉】+ 游戏名称 + 您的姓名/机构名称。

三、平台处理流程

  • 审核：我们在收到符合要求的通知后，将在 2 个工作日内进行初步审核。
  • 转通知：审核通过后，我们将立即将您的通知转送给被投诉游戏的投稿者/开发者。
  • 采取必要措施：依据《民法典》规定，我们将及时采取删除、屏蔽、断开链接等下架措施。
  • 反通知：若被投诉方提交了合规的反通知（证明其不侵权），我们将把反通知转送给您。若您未在合理期限内向有关部门投诉或提起诉讼，我们将恢复被下架的内容。

感谢您对了了了游知识产权保护工作的理解与支持。

Copyright Infringement Notice Guidelines

Last updated: October 18, 2026

lelele Game (the "Platform") highly values intellectual property protection. If you believe that a game displayed on the Platform infringes your rights, please submit a formal infringement notice according to these guidelines.

I. Notice Requirements

According to Article 1195 of the Civil Code of the PRC, your notice must contain:

  • Proof of Identity: Your name, ID/Business License copy, and contact info (phone, email, address).
  • Proof of Rights: Copyright registration certificates, trademark certificates, original drafts, or proof of first publication.
  • Location of Infringing Content: The specific name of the infringing game and its exact URL on our Platform.
  • Description of Infringement: A brief explanation of the allegedly infringing content and the reasons.
  • Statement of Truthfulness: A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the rights holder.
⚠️ Warning: If your notice is incomplete or non-compliant, the Platform will postpone processing. The rights holder shall bear legal liability for damages caused by false notices.

II. How to Submit

Please pack the above materials and send them to our designated email:

leleleling@2980.com

Please use the email subject: [Infringement Notice] + Game Name + Your Name/Organization.

III. Platform Processing Procedure

  • Review: We will conduct an initial review within 2 business days of receiving a compliant notice.
  • Forwarding: If approved, we will immediately forward your notice to the uploader/developer.
  • Taking Necessary Measures: In accordance with the Civil Code, we will promptly remove, block, or disable access to the content.
  • Counter-Notice: If the uploader submits a compliant counter-notice, we will forward it to you. If you do not file a complaint or lawsuit within a reasonable period, we will restore the removed content.

Thank you for your understanding and support for intellectual property protection at lelele Game.
